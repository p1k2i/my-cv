## Early Life, Education and Pre-IT Experience

I was born in Russia. After completing the first part of my schooling (9 years), I moved with my mother to Argentina. I studied in Córdoba and later moved to Buenos Aires where I completed adult education. While studying I worked various short-term jobs: construction (plastering, painting), furniture factory (assembly), and in restaurants (dishwashing, kitchen assistance, bicycle delivery).

I enrolled at UBA (University of Buenos Aires) and studied for about one year (partially completed exams), then continued studies at UTN (National Technological University) for roughly one year. I planned to specialize in electronic engineering but switched focus to practical work and IT before completing the degree.
